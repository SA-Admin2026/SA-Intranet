## Electronic Equipment and IT Policies

 

## Use of Facilities, Equipment and Property, Including Intellectual Property

 Equipment essential in accomplishing job duties is often expensive and may be difficult to replace. When using property, employees are expected to exercise care, perform required maintenance, and follow all operating instructions, safety standards and guidelines. Employees also are prohibited from any unauthorized use of the Company's intellectual property, such as audio and video tapes, print materials and software. Improper, careless, negligent, destructive, or unsafe use or operation of equipment can result in discipline, up to and including discharge. 

## Data Disposal Policy

 During your employment, the Company will collect certain information that is classified as “personal identifying information,” or PII, under applicable laws. Such information may include, but is not limited to: · Your first and last name or initials. · Username(s) and password(s). · Social security number. · Driver license or other identification card number. · Medical documentation. · Biometric data. · And more. The Company may keep these records in paper and/or electronic format. When such documentation is no longer needed, pursuant to records retention requirements and best practices, the Company will either (a) destroy the records or (b) arrange for their destruction, e.g. by shredding, erasing, or otherwise modifying the personal identifying information in such a manner as to render it unreadable or indecipherable through any means. 

## Communication Systems

 The communication and information systems are property of the Company and intended for business use. An employee’s use of these systems must not disrupt the operation of the Company network or the networks of other users. Moreover, it must not interfere with employees’ productivity. Personal use of the internet must be kept to a minimum. The Company maintains the ability to access any computer files, use of software, Internet usage, e-mail, and voice mail on company systems. Although employees may select individual passwords, employees should not assume that such files are confidential. However, other than management employees acting on behalf of the Company, employees should not attempt to gain access to another employee’s computer, Internet files, e-mail, or voice mail without the latter’s permission. All information regarding access to the Company’s computer resources, such as user identifications, modem phone numbers, access codes, and passwords is Company confidential information and may not be disclosed to non-Company personnel. Employees are expected to have access to a computer suitable for doing their work. We encourage personnel to have the appropriate equipment to do their jobs efficiently. In the event the employee needs to purchase a laptop or phone to perform their job appropriately, the employee can request the Company buy the device on behalf of the employee, by filling out our Device Request Form, (see our stand-alone Employee Device Purchase Request form) authorizing the following:
- The Company will provide software licenses for programs necessary for the execution of the employee’s duties. The employee will forfeit these licenses upon resignation or termination.
- The Company reserves the right to remove any information from the employee’s computer that the Company perceives as proprietary information, or intellectual property.
- The Company reserves the right to perform a security audit of the employee’s device at any time. 

## Software and Copyright The Company licenses and does not own most of the software it utilizes. Therefore, use of the software must be in accordance with the applicable Software Agreements or as directed by the Company. Employees must not use the Company’s technology resources to copy, retrieve, forward, or send copyrighted materials unless the employee has the author’s permission. 

## E-mail

 Because the Company provides the e-mail system to employees to help them with the performance of their job, it should be used for official Company business. Incidental and occasional personal use of e-mail is permitted; however, employees should be aware that these messages will be treated the same as business messages, and subject to review at any time without notice. Also, employees cannot control where their messages will end up. For example, a message meant for one person can be mistakenly sent to the wrong individual(s), or the message can be forwarded to unintended recipients. In addition, e-mails that were deleted are stored elsewhere on the system may be recovered/shared with other parties. Employees should use discretion when sending e-mails. Do not write anything in an e-mail message that is inappropriate to say to others face-to-face. The Company prohibits the display, transmittal, or downloading of material that is offensive, pornographic, obscene, profane, discriminatory, harassing, insulting, derogatory, or otherwise unlawful at any time. No one may solicit, promote, or advertise any outside organization, product, or service using e-mail or anywhere else on Company premises during working time. Working time does not include breaks or meal periods. Management may monitor e-mail from time to time. Employees are prohibited from unauthorized use of encryption keys or the passwords of other employees to gain access to another employee’s e-mail messages. 

## Telephones

 In the interest of good business practice, personal telephone calls, including those made with cell phones and mobile devices, must be minimal and not interfere with employees’ performance of their jobs. For safety reasons, employees should avoid the use of cell phones while driving. Employees are not permitted to text while operating a motor vehicle and can only text when the vehicle is at rest in the shoulder lane or lawfully parked. It is assumed that employees will use their own cell phones during their jobs. The Company does not provide reimbursement for cell phone usage or contribute to cell phone plans. Employees should ensure their voicemail greeting is professional and checked on a consistent basis in case of work-related contact. The Company does not have an “always on” policy. Unless a short-term exception is made for a specific project, there is no expectation that employees will return phone calls, emails, or texts from the end of one working day to the beginning of the next, or on the weekends. The general expectation is that communications are returned within 1 business day. 

## Personal Computers

 All employees will utilize their own computer for Semantic Arts related work. You may also be issued a client laptop for some projects, which should be used in accordance with client policies. Personal computers must: 1. Have an encrypted hard drive 2. Have installed and running anti-virus with a scan frequency of at least monthly 3. Have a system for regular backups of local data, either to an external drive or in the cloud. 4. Have some sort of login credentials required (fingerprint, passcode, etc.) 5. Be kept up to date with latest update package on OS and applications 6. Enable “Find My” services to locate and lock/unlock your device The use of a password management system is encouraged, but you should also set up the program to require authentication on a frequent basis. This helps to prevent unauthorized access into your password management system. 

## File Storage

 Generally, all Semantic Arts files must be stored on approved systems, which include local servers, SharePoint, Confluence, GitHub, the Shared Folders, and a few others. Local work files must be stored on an encrypted hard drive and should be uploaded into SA file structures as frequently as is feasible. Employees should generally avoid storing information in file systems outside of those approved by the company. This may be necessary at times but should be done with consideration for the security protocols of the system in question and whether or not the same goal can be accomplished within the Semantic Arts systems. 

## Potential Breaches Any employee who believes their computer has been compromised, lost or stolen must report to the Company immediately to protect system access. We will begin to “lock out” systems access and change passwords, as well as monitor for any breach of company information.
