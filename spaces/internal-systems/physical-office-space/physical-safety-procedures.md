# Physical Safety Procedures

In order to maintain safety and security onsite within the Opera Galleria, there are some guidelines related to who to call, and when.

The 2nd floor of the Opera Galleria, including staircases, are private property. Individuals not engaged in business on that floor or moving to the parking garage are trespassing.

**Individuals should be asked to leave once, and if they refuse or cause a disturbance, police should be notified immediately.**

**WHEN IN DOUBT, DIAL 911**

The 1st floor is considered to be public property, so there are no rules against loitering. It is however illegal to cause a disturbance, which can be addressed by notifying building security or Fort Collins Police. The nature of the disturbance along with the timeframe will determine who to contact. You generally can’t go wrong with Fort Collins non-emergency dispatch.

Where possible, it is requested that we attempt to gather video evidence of any illegal behavior. This evidence can be used by building management and the police to track and deal with repeat offenders. Do not put your personal safety at risk in order to record another individual.

Use the chart below for information about what agency to contact, dependent on the circumstances:

**LEVEL I:** Emergency with immediate intervention needed

*Assault, theft, threats, vandalism, or any situation in which you feel unsafe*

Call or **text**  911, give location and nature of help needed

**LEVEL II:** Non-Emergency police matter

Call FC Police at (970) 491-FCPD (3273) and select option 7 to go to dispatch

**LEVEL III:** Non-Urgent security concern

Call Precision Security at (970) 632-3696. Guard will be dispatched, could take 10-15 minutes to respond
