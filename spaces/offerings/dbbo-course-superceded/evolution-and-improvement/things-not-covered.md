# "Things not covered"

I think a slide for the final day that briefly lists some topics that we didn't cover, but which the student will likely encounter as they begin doing any work in semantics.  For example:

- Blank nodes
- Reification
- Named graphs

The point of the slide would be to remind them that we couldn't cover *everything* in 4 days, and that they shouldn't panic because these are well-documented things that they can find online assistance with.
