# Current Default Billing Rates

*Updated: 2025-11-08*

Billing rates are subject to the official COLA (cost-of-living adjustment) increase annually on January 1.

Current default billing rates by position are shown below. Project rates are for our standard knowledge graph implementation projects, and have minimal travel (domestic travel, once every six weeks) built in. Advisory rates are used for short-term, strategic projects generally requiring senior personnel. Negotiated rates for individual projects may vary from the default. Some clients, such as Morgan Stanley, use their own rate cards.
