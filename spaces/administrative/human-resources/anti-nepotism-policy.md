# Anti-Nepotism Policy

Semantic Arts does not hire relatives of current employees or members of the executive team. For purposes of this policy, “relatives” include spouses, domestic partners, parents, children, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren, in-laws, and other members of an employee’s household.

Applicants are required to disclose any familial relationship with a current employee during the hiring process. Failure to disclose such a relationship may result in disqualification from consideration or termination of employment.

We reserve the right to interpret and apply this policy at our sole discretion.
