# General Interviewing Pointers

Here are some general questions to ask, organized by the behavior you are attempting to learn more about:

![](../../attachments/2279211035/2278752294.png)![](../../attachments/2279211035/2279440419.png)

## SOAR (Behavioral Based Questions)

- **Situation:** Describe a situation in which you …
- **Objective and Obstacles:** What was your objective and what was in the way?
- **Action:** What action did you take?
- **Result:** What was the result?

## Questions you should never ask

- Generally, anything unrelated to the position, such as:

  - Are you married/have kids?
  - Who takes those kids to and from school?
  - Anything that could be construed as discriminatory based on age, race, gender, etc.
  - How old are you?
  - What is your religion
