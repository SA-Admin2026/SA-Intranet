SPARQL basics: asking the graph questions

SPARQL is how you query a knowledge graph. Instead of joining tables, you describe a pattern of triples — subject, predicate, object — and the engine finds every match. Because the graph already encodes meaning, you ask in business terms, not schema trivia.

Your first query

This returns every customer and the city they're located in:

PREFIX gist: <https://ontologies.semanticarts.com/gist/> SELECT ?customer ?city WHERE { ?customer a gist:Organization ; gist:isLocatedAt ?place . ?place gist:name ?city . }

Swap the pattern and you've asked a different question — no remodeling, because connect once, ask many times is the whole point of the data-centric approach.

Why this matters

An AI agent can introspect this same graph and answer in plain language — which is exactly the “semantic layer as the agent's interface” pattern from the trends brief.

New-hire setup

last updated 2026-07-22 · amanda.r · source: SA-Operations-Manual/people/new-hire-setup.md

This checklist covers everything needed before a new team member's first day. Owner: Operations. If anything's unclear, ping #ops.

Before Day 1

1. Create the Microsoft 365 account and add to the right Entra groups (all-staff + team + any project).
2. Order equipment and ship to arrive 2 days early.
3. Assign an onboarding buddy and add to the Week-1 calendar.

Day 1

Walk through accounts & MFA enrollment, the handbook, and the security-awareness training (linked in the LMS). Confirm payroll details in Paylocity.
