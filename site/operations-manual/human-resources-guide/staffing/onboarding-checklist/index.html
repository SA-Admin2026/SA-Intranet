Semantic Arts Onboarding & Separation Procedures

Section titled “Semantic Arts Onboarding & Separation Procedures”

Compliance & Administrative

Section titled “Compliance & Administrative”

First Steps

Section titled “First Steps”

  1. Background Check

    1. Initiate here: https://myhrscreens.bgsecured.com/sdocs/admin.html?announcements=1
    2. The system will send any required disclosures and request required information. You must select the relevant package, do not use the “a la carte” option. Returns typically within a day and will be notified via email.

  2. Rippling: Access Rippling and begin the process of onboarding a new employee. Ensure you send the required documents and be sure this process is completed in a timely fashion. Many of the other steps depend on this being done first.

  3. Employee Folder: Move all files from candidate folder to current employee folder (make sub-folder with “Recruiting” as title for their interview notes/resume)

Compliance Steps

Section titled “Compliance Steps”

  1. CO New Hire Reporting https://newhire.state.co.us/#/public/public-landing/login must be completed (regardless of which state they are employed in) within a few days of hire. Will need PII info.

  2. I-9 Form: Employee completes in Rippling. Schedule a meeting to visually verify their docs. Once done, you complete Employer portion in Rippling

    1. Found in “Employment Authorization” section within HR on Rippling

  3. E-Verify https://idp.uscis.gov/ Completed after I-9.

  4. State & Local Tax Requirements: Rippling will generate after their first payroll. You’ll need to set these up with the appropriate agencies and send info to Rippling

    1. Existing Account List: https://semarts.atlassian.net/wiki/x/CgDghw

Orientation Steps

Section titled “Orientation Steps”

Payroll

Section titled “Payroll”

Benefits

Section titled “Benefits”
  • Add to the Employee Navigator portal, send invite link where they will make elections
  • Human Interest: Confirm eligibility, they will get set up via Rippling. Maybe notify it will automatically start them at 4%
  • If they select a HDHP, they can set up an HSA. The best way is for them to set up an individual Optum account, then send me the account number so I can request it be added to our group.

Microsoft Office

Section titled “Microsoft Office”

Onboarding

Section titled “Onboarding”

Access the Office 365 admin portal, go to users and add a new user. Complete the following:

  • Set up a temporary password and email it to the new employee’s personal email
  • Apply the proper licenses for access
  • Add to the following distribution lists: Ontologists/Developers, SA Staff
  • Forward invites to relevant meetings

Allegrograph & Spark

Section titled “Allegrograph & Spark”

Add access at http://Agraph.semanticarts.com if appropriate (consultants only)

Add access to Spark. You should create the employee profile first. Right now, Jamie has to manually create a user account so email him to ask for that to be completed.

Email the employee, have them contact Jamie with issues.

Information on typical time charges is in the onboarding manual

Research/Job Training Resources

Section titled “Research/Job Training Resources”

Confluence

Section titled “Confluence”

Access the administrative section of Confluence and invite the new employee using their Semantic Arts email address (or revoke when terminating). This must be done before the next two items

  • Mark When Completed

Employee Handbook

Section titled “Employee Handbook”

Send the employee an email to notify them the employee handbook can be found on Confluence, and it is assumed you have read and understand what is in there.

  • Mark When Completed

Onboarding Resources

Section titled “Onboarding Resources”

Sent the employee to Onboarding Guidelines - Administrative - Confluence (atlassian.net) to review the onboarding process and read through getting started.

  • Mark When Completed

Expensify

Section titled “Expensify”

Access the system with an administrative username and send an invite to the new employee. Advise them to ask their Go-To Person for further assistance. Revoke when terminating.

  • Mark When Completed

Github

Section titled “Github”

  • Access http://www.github.com/semanticarts

  • Locate the option to invite a user

  • Purchase a new seat if none are available

  • Mark When Completed

Misc.

Section titled “Misc.”

Meetings

Section titled “Meetings”
  • Schedule a meeting on their 1st day to go over things
  • Schedule a meeting after about a week to review
  • Schedule a meeting at about a month to review and ask for feedback on process
  • Consider setting some intro calls with others on their related team, project, etc.

Staff Plan

Section titled “Staff Plan”
  • Add to staff plan (individual page, chart, and capacity) and assign projects accordingly (or remove).

Office Access

Section titled “Office Access”

If the new employee is local, provide them with an office key and hall key. Make sure they have the bathroom combination. Offer to provide information for garage parking if interested.

  • Mark When Completed

Swag

Section titled “Swag”
  • Order Business Cards from Vista Print using saved template (low quantity generally)
  • TagSwag is coming soon - Ask Dave about sending a gift card towards 1st purchase
  • Gift Card Request Form (monday.com) - TagSwag gift card sending form

Website Bio

Section titled “Website Bio”

Request the new employee create a short bio to match others on the website for addition. Also request a headshot that can be uploaded.

  • Mark When Completed

gist Team page (Github)

Section titled “gist Team page (Github)”
  • ONTOLOGY ONLY: Have them add name and github link to: Semantic Arts gist Team · semanticarts/gist Wiki (github.com)

Newsletter Add

Section titled “Newsletter Add”

Add the new employee to the Constant Contact newsletter list so they will receive SA newsletters

  • Mark When Completed

LinkedIn Post

Section titled “LinkedIn Post”
  • Post a welcome message on company LinkedIn page

Go-To-Person

Section titled “Go-To-Person”

Set up a go-to person that should be a newer SA employee who remembers what it was like to be new, and someone working in the same type of position the new hire is in. Set up a meeting between the two for the first day of work

  • Mark When Completed

Coach/Mentor

Section titled “Coach/Mentor”

Assign a coach (generally at the next coaches meeting). Send an email introduction to the employee and their coach. On separation, if the person is a coach you will need to reassign those assigned to their team

  • Mark When Completed

Section titled “Staff Updates”

Onboarding

Section titled “Onboarding”

  • Introduce to the staff: Dave often does at a staff meeting, but can be done in an email or other creative way as well

  • Email Steve Case with the new employee’s resume and contact information

  • Mark When Completed

Separation

Section titled “Separation”

  • Set up an exit interview and use the form found in the shared folders

  • Mark When Completed

Misc. Subsidiary Specific Tasks

Section titled “Misc. Subsidiary Specific Tasks”

  • Research and process specific forms needed for each region

  • Maintain record of forms, subsidiary details

  • Canada Health Insurance – Sunlife, need to process application for new hires in CAN

  • UK Health Insurance – National Insurance, automatically deducted from pay

  • Setup applicable tax accounts for new regions, research local labor laws

  • Mark When Completed
