<http://vocbench.uniroma2.it/>

VocBench is a capable tool for managing teams and collaboratively building SKOS, SKOS-XL, OWL, RDF and Ontolex semantic assets. It also includes a really useful tool (Sheet2RDF) for converting square Excel sheets to RDF using some automatic recognition of the spreadsheet columns and an extraction and mapping language (PEARL) described in <http://art.uniroma2.it/sheet2rdf/>

VocBench is actively supported by UN/FAO (where it has been used for years to manage AGROVOC) and by the European Union Publications Office where it is the tool of choice for managing EUROVOC and other taxonomic assets.

It is also useful for creating and managing lexical schemas - <http://vocbench.uniroma2.it/doc/user/ontolex_editing.jsf>
