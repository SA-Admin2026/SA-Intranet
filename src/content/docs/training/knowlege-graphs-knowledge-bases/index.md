What is the difference between an **Ontology**, a **Knowledge Base**, and a **Knowledge Graph**?

- An ontology is the definition of the domain.  It can be compared to a database schema.
- A knowledge base is a collection of graph data (concepts linked by predicates) that constitute your raw source of information.
- A knowledge graph is the combination of both.  It allows you to extract additional knowledge from your knowledge base, such as inferred relations and enrichment of existing concepts and predicates.
