*Parti* is a term borrowed from the building architecture industry.  It refers to sketches that communicate the key organizing principles and themes of a building design process.  We have found that most ontology designs go through a stage where the key organizing principles emerge.

An ontology parti is not a full design, but is sufficient to convey to the client the essentials of the domain area being developed.  Neither is the parti "carved in stone". It is intended to elicit feedback and corrections.
