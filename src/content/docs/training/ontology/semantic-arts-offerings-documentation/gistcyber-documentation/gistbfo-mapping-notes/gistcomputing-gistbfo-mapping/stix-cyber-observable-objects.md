The concept of cyber observable objects is best addressed in the Cyber Threat Intelligence Ontology and NOT the gistCyber ontology. Most, if not all of the STIX Cyber Observable (SCO) objects, map to the gistComputing concepts.  
  
Hence, the SCOs are being relocated to the CTIO. At his time, I don’t know the role that Categories will come into play for SDOs, SCOs, SROs, and SMOs.
