A *distinctionary* is a glossary in which the term definitions first describes what the item is, and then describes how it is different from others of that type.  These definitions are both more precise and useful, and also lend themselves to easier creation of formal OWL definitions.

This is essentially the same idea as [Genus differentia definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus%E2%80%93differentia_definition).
