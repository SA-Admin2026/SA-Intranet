[SEMIC2023.eu](http://SEMIC2023.eu) - the conference of the EU semantic interoperability community that was in Madrid at the start of Spain’s Presidency of the EU focused on the use of AI in semantic interoperability. The key policy background to this includes the [Interoperable Europe Act 2022](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2023/10/06/interoperable-europe-act-member-states-agree-common-position-to-deliver-more-efficient-digital-public-services-across-the-eu) and from this stem the following:

- policy alignment and a fixed scope for interoperability work
- mandatory interoperability assessments
- alignment with the [Artificial Intelligence Act](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/headlines/society/20230601STO93804/eu-ai-act-first-regulation-on-artificial-intelligence)
- A governance framework and board to ensure alignment across Member States
- sharing and reuse of solutions, often open source, powered by an ‘Interoperable Europe Portal' – a one-stop-shop for solutions and community cooperation.

What does this mean for Semantic Arts? There will be in the coming years a huge amount of interoperability work in public and private organisations across the EU. To get engaged with this we need to be aware of what is going on, and also to reduce barriers to engagement - this may include setting up a subsidiary within the EU (perhaps within the Estonian e-Residency programme). The principal function of attending the conference was to get the company onto peoples' radar and to publicise the gist ontology.

The report by the [Joint Research Centre](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/index_en) (JRC) is “[Artificial Intelligence for interoperability in the European public sector - An exploratory study](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/3f94b728-640c-11ee-9220-01aa75ed71a1/language-en)”

An acronym that will become more visible in coming years is “AI4IOP” - AI for interoperability in the public sector. As with environmental legislation, once the interoperability requirements become embedded in policy there will be a push to implement these along the supply chain, and the absence of interoperability characteristics (these will be determined by the tests of the mandatory assessments mentioned above) will preclude businesses from certain markets. So, I see this set of policies as being a significant driver for potential business for SA and others

The development of TEFs - [Testing Europe Facilities](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/activities/testing-and-experimentation-facilities) - is where AI solutions will be evaluated for complying with the requirements of the Artificial Intelligence Act as mentioned above.
