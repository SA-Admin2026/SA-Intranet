Thinking about areas of semantic technology and ontology that are either underdeveloped, poorly integrated or both (i.e., 'slums').  The more I think about it actually the more I think that the only part that's really good is storage.  Ultimately, we should come up with opportunities for how to improve these things (either by coming up with a better solution or making two things more integrated).

![](/attachments/5144627/5144626.png)

### R2RML + RDF Shapes

This is a big opportunity for us since we're probably among the few people who have a lot of experience in both.  My idea is to 'seed' an R2RML editor with an RDF shapes document – once you've done that you only have to create a logical table and select columns (or uri templates) for each of the values.  If nothing else this would allow us to do something on the scale of the Clipsal ETL in a matter of hours or days, instead of weeks.
