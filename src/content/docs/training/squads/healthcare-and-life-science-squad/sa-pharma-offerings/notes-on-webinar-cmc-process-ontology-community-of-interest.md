The Problem Statement covered:

- Data is collected electronically in notebooks, but often not easily searchable or extractable.

  - Data is embedded in Excel Spreadsheets, PowerPoint, or PDF.
- Getting data from the lab/plant into a regulatory files is still a manual and inefficient process that requires many human hours.
- We are on a digital transformation journey and focusing on the CMC space to automate and accelerate this process.
- Our major asset in CMC is the data we produce:

  - CMC data defines our manufacturing processes
  - CMC data defines our medical product profile
- How we describe our data is key to leveraging it effectively

  - Primary uses:

    - Decision making
    - Process and product definition for tech transfer
    - Regulatory submissions
  - Secondary uses:

    - Prior knowledge
    - Predictive science (AI / ML)
    - Digital Twins

PHASE 2:

The scope of phase 2 is to extend the PoC Pharmaceutical CMC Process Ontology into a comprehensive CMC process ontology for chemical, mAb, and CAR-T manufacturing. Included in this expansion is a focus on ensuring there is interoperability with key related ontologies such as IDMP-O, Analytical, Instrument, and Equipment.

The target outcome that proofs value is the implementation of pilot process recipes in vendor software and validated ontology effectiveness through competency questions using sample data.
