### Background

The first priority of this project, as determined within the internal Semantic Arts Summit in Fort Collins is to develop an offering around the use case of Disease Target Identification (or broader) as shown below. Disease Target Identification is defined in the following 2 webpages [[Example 1](https://www.enzo.com/biopharma/discovery/target-identification/)] [[Example 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5543995/)].

![Unknown-3.png](/attachments/2992078853/2991620106.png)

There seems to be a ton of open data around this, and adjacent use cases around drug repurposing.
