Process Validation v/s Quality Control and Testing:

1. QC/Testing is done every single time we manufacture a material, its mandatory to release the material for next phase of its use (i.e. as starting material for next phase of manufacturing or commercial distribution)
2. Validation is done once to ensure that the process we have proposed to manufacture the product/material is able to consistently produce the material which meets the specification during it QC/Testing, if validation study fails to produce acceptable quality of material, its revalidated with stricter controls on process parameters/indicators to ensure it passes before a process can be used for commercial manufacturing

Control Strategy:

1. The ideal form of the product/drug that would give its intended therapeutic effect is controlled through establishing various controls for a material's Quality Attribute over its development, manufacturing and release lifecycle phases 1. Attributes that impact clinical performance of a drug is designated critical
2. Example of a developmental control are controls established thorough characterization and validation of process and product formulation
3. Example of a manufacturing control are in-process controls and critical in-process controls established as part of process design
4. Example of a release control is specifications for materials that are setup for qualifying lots for its next phase of use through Quality Control Testing  
   No one probably classifies controls in these ways (but it helps me to understand why they are different and when along the development lifecycle they help in establishing product quality)
