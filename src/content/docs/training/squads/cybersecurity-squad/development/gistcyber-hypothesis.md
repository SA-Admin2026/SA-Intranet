# The Scientific Method

The Hypothesis is a concept that is not represented in gistCore at the present time. I strongly believe that it should be. Why do we form plans to do something? A simplistic answer is that we want the result of the planned process. We expect something if the plan is executed. That expectation could be stated as a Hypothesis. A Hypothesis might be that I expect that I’ll have a cake if I execute the “make a cake plan”. If the result of executing the plan is not a cake, then we must form an updated plan

The Scientific Method is presented in this [slide show](https://www.slideserve.com/ophira/the-scientific-method) with plenty of examples.

One could think of the hypothesis as the experimental plan. If the plan is executed the result will confirm or reject the hypothesis.
