This portal is the firm's *Technical & Research Resources* — 240+ lessons migrated out of Confluence into open Markdown we own. Because the content is now structured data, it does more than a wiki: it's **searchable**, **editable in place**, **navigable by learning path**, tracks **your progress**, and surfaces the **connections between topics** as a live map.

<ProgressOverview />

<Card title="See how it all connects">
  The lessons carry 340+ cross-references from Confluence. The [knowledge map](/map/) renders them as an interactive graph — the kind of connected view that was impossible when everything lived on flat wiki pages.
</Card>

## How to use this

- **Pick a learning path** below or from the sidebar — start with *New ontologist orientation*.
- **Mark lessons complete** as you go; the progress bars above and the sidebar checkmarks update automatically (saved in your browser).
- **Follow the "related lessons"** at the bottom of each page, or open the [knowledge map](/map/) to explore visually.
- **Search** anything with the search box up top.
