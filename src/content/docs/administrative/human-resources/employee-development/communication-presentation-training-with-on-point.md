\*Note: I included my notes about slide decks in the SA Presentation Template, so won’t duplicate them here

**A story is 22x more powerful than a fact - wrap each fact in a story**

**H**ook - draw them in quickly

**O**rient - Sit in the muck with them…why the current situation is bad.

This is the hardest part and requires an “onramp” to help get everyone up to speed

**W**in - how we do it (look under the hood) and our value proposition

**T**ell

**O**ffer - offer the audience a way in

**Practicing and Rehearsal**

Practice for yourself, rehearse for others (maybe 3-7 optimal)

*Shortcut: Record the perfect version of the presentation and listen back to it*
