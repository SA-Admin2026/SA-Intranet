The purpose of this email is to ask candidates to do some self rating before the technical interview. This will allow the interviewer to tailor the call to the skillset and come in with a good baseline estimation of where a candidate may fall.

Hello,

Based on feedback from your previous calls, we would like to move you forward into a more technical interview. This would be roughly 45 minutes with a member of our ontology team. **In this interview, please be prepared to share your screen while working through questions/exercises.**

As I mentioned in our initial call, this interview is not so much "pass/fail" as it is determining your current level of knowledge with our toolset. Specifically, we are looking to determine abilities to write and understand RDF/S, OWL and SPARQL. Other technologies that could be discussed might be SHACL, python, or other technical abilities you mention in your resume. Even if you are not overall advanced in these skills, we will use the interview to attempt to gauge your overall understanding of concepts and theory and propensity to scale your capabilities.

In order to schedule, please send me an email estimating your overall competency with the following:

- RDF/S
- OWL
- SPARQL
- SHACL
- Python or other programming language (indicate which)

\*For each, indicate **None, Beginning, Intermediate, Advanced,** or **Expert.**

I will use this information as the baseline for the interviewer to shape questions.

Please let me know if you have any questions or concerns.

Thanks,
