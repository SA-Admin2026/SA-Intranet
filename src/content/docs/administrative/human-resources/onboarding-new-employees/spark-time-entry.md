## Create Your Account

New employees will need to create an account in the Spark system.

- Go to <https://spark.semanticarts.com/>.
- Click the button that says “LOG-IN”.
- A login window should pop up.
- Click the “sign up” link at the bottom.
- Enter your Semantic Arts email address and create a new password.
- Click “Create Account”.
- You must verify your email address before you can login.
- Go to your Semantic Arts email and look for an email, open the email and click the link to verify your email address.
- Then go back to <https://spark.semanticarts.com> and login.
- If you were set up correctly before hand, you should see your “Me” page that show a little information about you.

## Tracking Your Work Hours

Once you have access to the Spark system, you’ll need to start entering your time into the system.

Ideally, your time should be entered as close to when it actually occurred as possible. Practically, this is often the end of day. Try not to let your time entry get away from you - it’s harder to remember what you did days later.

There are at a basic level two types of time charges - internal and external. You should only be able to charge time to internal projects, as well as any client work you are assigned to. For internal time charges, here are a few common categories and when you might use them:

**Employee Engagement - Being Coached or Mentored** is good for any meetings with a coach

**Employee Engagement - Staff Meeting** is the proper place for Tuesday and Friday staff meetings, as well as gist Forum or Estes Park Group

**Employee Productivity - Todo mgmt, Planning, Email, Chat** is a bit of a catch-all bucket for time spent generally keeping up or dealing with admin tasks

**Employee PTO:** Use the appropriate charge for general vacation, sick time, holiday, etc.

**Improve Offerings - gist Core:** Include *only* time spent working on gistCore and attending the bi-monthly gist development team meetings. Do not include work on sub-gists, gist reference data, presentations about gist, gist Forum attendance, etc. Some of these items are captured in other tasks; otherwise use the generic Improve Offerings task.

**Onboarding & Offboarding - New Hire - Trainee** for time spent onboarding and being trained on Semantic Arts systems and processes

**Skills Upgrade - Being Trained** is more for training sessions or other learnings once you are on the team but continuing to develop your abilities and skills

Everyone can feel free to add others here you think would benefit from being highlighted or better described!

*One additional note: You will see some “Unpaid” tasks in the timecard. A few people prefer the ability to track all their time in a day, including activity that isn’t paid by the company. There is no requirement to do this, only personal preference. Please note that any time you put into these categories is truly unpaid - it won’t show up in the queries or ultimately your paycheck!*
