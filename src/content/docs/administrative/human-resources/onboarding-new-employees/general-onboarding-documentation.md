## **First Things First**

Before we get too far, there will be a couple of primary steps:

1. Onboard in Rippling: This is essentially our onboarding and payroll tool. You will receive an invite to set up an account and they will walk you through the required documents. Much of this information is also essential for the HR processes required for new employees, so taking care of it first helps us stay in compliance with these laws.
2. Complete a background check: This is required both internally, but also for many of our clients, so nice to get it out of the way early. You will get an authorization and info sheet via Rippling, please fill this out first thing so we can get started promptly.
3. Employee Email Address: Watch for an email to your personal account to set up an Office 365 account. This will provide access to the suite of Microsoft tools, as well as your company email account. All remaining onboarding information will be emailed here, so you’ll need access to move forward.

## **Programs**

There are a handful of programs and applications you can expect to use. You will receive invitations to sign up or register. They include:

- Confluence: You are already here, congrats!
- Expensify: For tracking personal expenses while traveling for Semantic Arts in order to receive reimbursement (look for email to set up in your SA inbox)
- SharePoint/OneDrive (different names for the Microsoft online document store): Primary source for documents. Go to <https://semarts.atlassian.net/l/cp/Y8AjWffe> to get access
- [Spark](https://spark.semanticarts.com): This is our internal management tool. Currently it is used for time tracking and project management. You’ll need to create an account with your SA email, this can be done near the bottom of the login pop-up dialog. See the [Spark Time Entry](spark-time-entry.md) page for instructions. Contact Jamie ([jamie.gulden@semanticarts.com](mailto:jamie.gulden@semanticarts.com)) for troubleshooting.
- Technical employees:

  - Github - You’ll get an invite in your SA inbox and can then link a personal account or set up a new one. This is where we work on our gist ontologies and is sometimes used for client work.
  - Allegrograph - This is our main local RDF triplestore, available for learning and client work.

## Misc - Accessing Email on Your Phone

1. Tap "Settings", go to "Mail, Contacts, Calendars", then "Add Account"
2. You should see a list of well known email providers. Tap "Microsoft Exchange"
3. Enter your email address and password in the appropriate input fields. Provide a name for your own reference (such as Semantic Arts) in the "Description" field.
4. If you are directed to a screen that prompts for more information (such as Email address, Server, Domain, etc.):

   1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field
   2. Enter [outlook.office365.com](http://outlook.office365.com) in the "Server" field
   3. Leave the "Domain" field blank
   4. Enter the name you use to login to your email (likely your email address) in the "Username" field
   5. Enter your password in the "Password field"

- If anything is autofilled in this screen after entering your email address and password in the preceding one, you should be able to leave all the form data as it is
