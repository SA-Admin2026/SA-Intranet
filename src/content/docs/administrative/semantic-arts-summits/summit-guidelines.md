Please refer to the guidelines below on Semantic Arts Summits. These will help to ensure two things:

1. We can control costs in such a way that it remains feasible for everyone to gather twice a year in Fort Collins as we grow
2. Summits are enjoyable and productive for everyone involved

## Financial Considerations

- Generally, flying will be the best option. You will be reimbursed for your airfare. You should plan airfare according to the client travel guidelines at [Travel & Expense Policy](../company-reference-guide/travel-expense-policy.md)

  - Should you choose to drive, you must track your mileage for the trip and will be reimbursed at the current IRS mileage rate.

    - NOTE: For those that choose to drive, should the cost come out higher than the cost of airfare you will only be reimbursed up to the airfare cost. Please find multiple flights to compare against and send JT those comparisons for reference with your expenses.
- Should the company reserve a block of hotel rooms, you are not required to stay there however will only be reimbursed for another room at the same or lower rate.
- For meals, you will be reimbursed for actual expenses, not per diem. Some meals will be provided while in town, but for those you are responsible for please do your best to keep them reasonable
- Some have inquired about the ability to bring family members along. This is allowable with a few caveats

  - You are responsible for attending all summit sessions. Family should make alternative arrangements while you are working
  - You cannot request reimbursement for any expenses related to family (airfare, meal costs, etc.)
  - Should there be any extra events paid for by the company, family may be allowed to attend at their own cost. Speak with JT beforehand to confirm should this arise.
- Reimbursements will be processed through Expensify as usual. Please include supporting receipts for each expense
- In any case where you are substituting expenses (airfare vs. vehicle, lodging, etc) - you will be reimbursed the **lesser** of the actual, or what it would have been. For example, if you choose to stay somewhere other than the hotel booked that is actually cheaper, you will be reimbursed the real expense, not what the hotel would have been.

## Other Considerations

- Please keep in mind you are responsible for following the code of conduct and employee expectations listed in the employee manual at all times while in town.
- If you have any questions, concerns, or thoughts to make these summits better, let JT know!
