# Federal Government Sales

[SAM.gov | Entity Workspace](https://sam.gov/workspace/em/entities/non-federal)

![image-20240116-183712.png](/administrative/attachments/2494660609/2494234645.png)

We are also registered on various state websites to receive RFP announcements. These generally direct to the office manager email. We have a few other federal contract emails, as well as the BidMatch program which go to JT and office manager that alert to new opportunities.

Registration on [SAM.gov](http://SAM.gov) is done annual and must be kept up to date, typically as a minimum requirement to get paid via a federal contract.
