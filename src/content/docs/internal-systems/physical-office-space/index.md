Fort Collins, CO 80524

## Parking

Available in the adjacent garage by the hour or by permit. Semantic Arts has two company permits, individuals can purchase on a monthly basis as needed.

You can also park on the streets around Old Town, but be aware of time restrictions and meter requirements. The Company will not be responsible for parking tickets received.

## Restrooms

A code or key is required to access the restrooms. Do not distribute the code or provide access for anyone not an employee or guest of the Company.

The code is 13579# for both men’s and women’s
