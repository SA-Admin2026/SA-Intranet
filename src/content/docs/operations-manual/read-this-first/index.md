## Introduction

Welcome to the operations & management manual for Semantic Arts. While this guide cannot be all-encompassing, the goal is to distill processes and procedures into one central repository to enable efficiency and accuracy in each jurisdiction.

Within this section you will find a breakdown of some key to do list items. I would recommend you utilize some type of to do app (I prefer Trello) to track those that are under your area of management. Recurring tasks help remember the things that might slip through the cracks otherwise. I believe this job is best done when you “remember nothing”…that is, write everything down and don’t rely on your memory later.

## Change Management

Generally, this manual is going to be a living document that should be modified and updated when appropriate. The following are guidelines for how we will handle changes:

| **Change Type** | **Approval** |
| --- | --- |
| Formatting, Grammar, Spelling | None - just do it! |
| Process Improvement | Create comments either on Confluence or on separate document to share within the appropriate administrative team members and decide together on changes. |
| Policy or official procedure | These must be approved by leadership. Write up a separate document with suggested changes and why you are suggesting them. |
