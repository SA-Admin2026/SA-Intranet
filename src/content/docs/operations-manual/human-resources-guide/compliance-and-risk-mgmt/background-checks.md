Background checks must be conducted when onboarding a new employee, or sometimes as a directive from a client as part of the consultant onboarding process.

Our background check provider is currently ADP. JT has credentials saved in LastPass

Before conducting a background check, you must give the employee a copy of their rights according to the Fair Credit Report Act. You will also need them to sign the authorization form and provide some additional information. This can be done via Rippling for transmission and saving if desired.

Once you have that completed, log in to ADP, generally background reports should include the following:

* ADP Crim Radar
* Local (State, County, City?)
* SSN Verification
* FACIS Level I
* Order Builder

Sometimes, clients will require additional levels of screening. Review onboarding documents carefully to ensure you meet their minimum standards.

Checks typically take a few days to come back.

If you receive a negative result, you are required to disclose that to an applicant who is denied a job due to their background check and give them an opportunity to contest the result.

Keep in mind results are not 100% accurate and further research should be done to ensure correct information should a negative result be returned.
