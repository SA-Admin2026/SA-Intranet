Verification of employment is most frequently completed when an employee purchases or refinances a home and the lender requires information. You should not release employee information without having them fill out the form referenced above first.

Sometimes a letter will be required, other times a specific form. Below are some guidelines

* Include date of employment and current position from Rippling
* For probably of continued employment: “No reason to question continuation (Company Policy)
* Current Gross Base Pay:

Our consultants are paid an ongoing bonus based on their chargability and billing rate.  At \_\_\_\_\_\_\_’s position his base is $\_\_\_/ hour.  Based on other consultants in his position we expect him to soon be at a sustained rate of $50-$55/hour (this might be modified if they are averaging - then use current base)

* Location: Remote from home

**NOTE: Authorization to release information as well as personalized VOE letter should be stored in the employee’s file, not in this location. You can search employee files for an older version to use as a template.**
