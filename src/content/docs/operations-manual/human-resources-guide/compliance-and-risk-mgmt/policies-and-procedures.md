Frequency: At least annual

An HR manager should work to stay on top of local, state, and national laws that change and therefore affect the policies and procedures of Semantic Arts. There may also be internal changes that necessitate revision.

Current policies and procedures are all found in the “Administration” page within Confluence. There are three types of reviews that could happen

1. HR Review - where you go through and review policies for appropriate updates and changes uni-laterally
2. Legal Review - communication with our retained legal services to determine if policies need changed or if you become aware of new laws that may cause us to be non-compliant
3. Management Review - Where Dave or other senior leadership offer modifications to existing policy, or you have thoughts on a change that must first be confirmed by Dave.

   1. Note: This should be the practice if making any substantive changes to existing policy

By posting policies and procedures online, there is an inherent assumption the staff has read and is therefore accountable to that information. If you determine an employee to breach one of these policies, reference them back to the handbook.
