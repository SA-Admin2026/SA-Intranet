## **Application Process - Ontologist**

Part I: Application Submitted

* May come from a variety of sources but should always include a Resume and ideally LinkedIn profile
* Need to also fill out standard application, currently <https://forms.office.com/r/KNmz0PJ3Ea>

Part II: Check References - As Needed (not really doing this in general)

* Can you verify that you were the [title of contact or relationship] for [Candidate Name]?
* If given the opportunity would you work with [Candidate Name] again?
* Please list one strength and one opportunity that you noticed while working with [Candidate Name].

Part III: Schedule First Phone Screen

* Once the candidate has completed the generic application, send an email requesting a meeting if they seem to be a potentially good fit. I use Calendly scheduling to allow the candidate to select a time on my calendar that works to avoid the back and forth
* During the phone screen:

  + During the administrative phone screen, the goal is to assess the candidate’s basic requirements and to identify any immediate issues the candidate may have in meeting the basic requirements to work at Semantic Arts.
  + See the phone screen template in the HR folder for a current list of questions
  + Feel free to deviate if you feel there are areas that require extra attention, just don’t venture into any questions regarding protected classes or that could be construed as discrimination

Part IV: Ontologist Interviews

* If successful in phone screen, ask the candidate to send some availability to schedule with a member of the consulting team
* Utilize questions found in Confluence page on interview questions
* Request they return notes to you regarding recommendation of moving forward
* Generally, a candidate will do two separate interviews with ontologists, unless an ontologist feels strongly about not recommending a candidate move forward

Part V: Audition

* Verify with Dave if you are unsure about moving a candidate forward
* Reach out for availability and let them know the following

  + Expect about two hours with a small team of both junior and senior ontologists. We like to have a cross section of people to ensure a wide range of perspectives.
  + Expect some modeling, so please have a pen and paper handy (dark pen such as sharpie that will show in camera is best)
* Work to schedule the two hour audition - if possible include Dave and 3 additional ontologists. Use a senior person if Dave is unavailable along with 3 others to target 4 total ontologists and HR in the interview

  + Pay attention to diversity - try to include both genders and a cross-section of ages
* After scheduled, email candidate NDA and request they sign before interview
* Use Dave’s “Notes about the audition” as a template for the role play portion
* Schedule a review session as soon after the audition as possible

  + Include everyone that was involved in the interview process, as well as Steve Case as optional
* If extending an offer, Dave may put together or you might, just check with him
* If not extending an offer, send candidate an email

  + Keep generic, don’t get into specifics
  + “Decided not to move forward with an offer, but we wish you the best of luck”
  + May be a bit candidate specific as well but don’t offer details
