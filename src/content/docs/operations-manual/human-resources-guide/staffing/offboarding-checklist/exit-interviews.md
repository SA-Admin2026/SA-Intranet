Exit Interviews should be conducted whenever an employee separates from the company. Generally, try to complete on their last day of work.

Material should include a combination of logistical questions as well as questions intended to determine why they left and what Semantic Arts can improve upon in the future.

Keep questions open-ended and conversational where you can. Give the employee a chance to share, even if not on topic to the questions listed.
