## **HSA**

* If an employee takes advantage of the benefits plan that includes the HSA they can open a bank account with Optum bank and provide us with the account info for direct deposit or elected amount.

  + Use deduction spreadsheet to help keep track.
* It is possible, though a bit of a grey area, for an employee with a HDHP through another avenue (such as state exchange) to set up an HSA and receive pre-tax contributions. I wouldn’t advertise this, but if asked it’s probably safe to allow.

## **Payroll Reconciliation**

* Monthly premiums should be deducted from 12 payrolls starting in August
* On the final payroll before the end of the plan year, there will be no standard deductions but you should use this period to reconcile previous year invoices with deductions

  + Review invoices for health insurance and dental coverage from last August to current July
  + Compare to the total deductions withheld from each enrolled participant
  + If not 0, then set up payroll to adjust accordingly

    - Email the employee to notify them of any charges being applied

## **COBRA**

* Employees who have paid into the employer provided group health, vision and/or dental plan are eligible for COBRA upon separation.
* Semantic does not pay any part of the coverage rates.
* Consult the most recent List Bill from Sage Benefit to determine the monthly coverage rate
* For Administrator:

  + To calculate rate, use the following formula:

    - Total rate x 13 months = Annual cost / 12 months = Monthly cost to individual for health
    - Add vision and health as needed
  + Insurance is charged on a 12-month basis. The last month, July, is reconciliation period.
