Frequency: Occassional

A few times a year or as Dave requests, you should attempt to find comparable salary offerings to those of Semantic Arts. This can be accomplished a few days

* Salary Surveys: Often these cost money and rarely include Ontologists, so be wary
* Search LinkedIn, Indeed, etc for other Ontologist openings and see if they list salary totals
* [Occupation Keyword Search (onetonline.org)](https://www.onetonline.org/find/result?s=it+consultant)

The goal is to determine if Semantic Arts is offering a competitive wage for each position. Keep in mind we are usually a bit heavier on gross compensation, and a bit lower on benefit offerings. You want to do your best to balance these two and get a holistic view of whether or not we are losing candidates/employees for this reason or not.
