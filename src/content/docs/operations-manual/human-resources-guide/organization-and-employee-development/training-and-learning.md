**NOTE:** It is the consultant’s responsibility to make sure they communicate clearly when they use or increase their T&L budget. I like to remind them occassionally that this is true, but it is also stated in the policy - you can’t log what you don’t know happened.

There are two procedures related to the training and learning policy

**Procedure 1: When an employee uses budget**

This is when someone makes a purchase using the T&L budget. Might be for a class, conference book, etc.

1. Log the purchase in the T&L Tracking document as a negative against the budget
2. Reimburse the expense via Rippling and place hardcopy in Dave’s folder

If an employee begins to approach $0 or overspends their budget, notify Dave to determine next steps

**Procedure 2: When an employee increases budget**

This happens when an employee speaks at a conference or other event and receives an increase in their yearly budget. It is the employee’s responsibility to notify the office of the event and the number of attendees/leads generated

1. Gather any additional information needed from the employee
2. Enter into the [T&L Tracking](https://app.box.com/s/j7ucz1f5jh4s5llzdlbmsjgmoxsy9ze2) spreadsheet under their tab, making sure it increases the budget appropriately

   1. If there are expenses associated with the event (most common with conferences), you should subtract that from the total and reimburse those expenses as in procedure 1.

There can be potential for a bonus at the end of the year if an employee has more than $3,000 remaining in their training budget. See the employee handbook for details on how this works, and make sure to coordinate with Dave if this looks to be the case.
