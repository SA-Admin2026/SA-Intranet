Performance reviews are conducted when an employee’s conduct or job performance is below acceptable levels. These are usually handled by Dave or other leaders, but HR must carefully document the process using the form found above.

If a performance review includes steps required for correction, it may be your responsibility to ensure future improvement goals are met.

Once you have filled out a PIP document, the file as well as all supporting documentation should be filed within the employee’s individual folder. You should work to save all documents that support the PIP, including emails, written reports of conversations, etc. These may be essential should possible litigation arise in the future.
