Company wide meetings are generally “owned” by [office@semanticarts.com](mailto:office@semanticarts.com). This includes staff meetings, knowledge exchange, sales team (Thurs), gist Development, and guest speakers. Use this account to manage changes/cancellations.

1. Monday Resource Planning (Steve Case owns)
2. Tuesday Staff Meeting
3. Tuesday Ontology Knowledge Exchange
4. gist Development (2x month on Thursday)
5. Sales/New Leads Thursday
6. Friday Staff Meeting
7. Friday Guest Speakers

Tuesday Meeting always includes hours breakdown, ER, etc

Friday meeting is always pipeline, only includes hours breakdown on payroll weeks (only hours, not ER and other things)
