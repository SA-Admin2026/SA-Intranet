There are two types of government reporting: payroll filings and misc taxes.

* Payroll filings are handled via Rippling and done at time of onboarding. Should you receive a letter from a tax agency, especially related to withholding, SUI or similar, you should scan the document and send to Rippling to determine the appropriate next steps

  + When setting up new tax accounts, try to use general company information wherever possible (office email vs personal, office phone vs cell phone, etc.). You may also need some personal info for Dave - this can be found in Rippling under his profile
* Tax Reports: Most items in this category are occasional or one-time, so it’s impossible to have procedures covering each. Currently this is limited to Washington DES, Minnesota, and Crown Commerial. All three are $0 activity so fairly simple, but would pick up if we do extensive enough business in one state to need to begin filing

# Sales Tax Reporting

## Washington

### DES Report

* [Login (wa.gov)](https://apps.des.wa.gov/CSR/login.aspx)
* Select 08215 - Information Technology Professional Services
* Select Current Year
* Select Current Quarter

Q1 Ends March 31, due April 30

Q2 ends June 30, due July 31

Q3 ends September 30, due October 21

Q4 ends December 31, due January 31 of next year

## Report Crown Commercial sales for taxes

* [Report management information to CCS (crowncommercial.gov.uk)](https://www.reportmi.crowncommercial.gov.uk/)
* Report Monthly
* Must be used if we win a UK RFP, but otherwise just reporting $0
