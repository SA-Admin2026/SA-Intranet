***GENERAL NOTES***

1. Need a venue that can accommodate the whole staff, as well as catering of meals for the length of the summit

   1. Carpool plan if required
   2. Meals can be on your own, but venue must be in a location to allow for walking distance to meals and enough time allotted for such things (maybe an hour 15 min?)
2. Need some recreational activities for team building

   1. A team dinner is nice - we used the Rio in 2024 and was good for laid back nature and ability to move around and not sit at a single long table
   2. Something active fun too - previously axe throwing, cooking class, walking tours, etc.
3. Need an itinerary of discussion topics that apply to ontologists, developers, and admin staff

   1. This takes a while to iterate, ask staff for ideas and will take a few drafts with Dave
4. Probably some type of HR updates, insurance (if timing is right), etc.

   1. Dave will always do a State of the Union to kick things off
5. Logistical items

   1. Coffee/Water during the day
   2. Whiteboard
   3. Projector and screen
   4. Internet, power cords
