Often, companies or individuals will be brought up for presenting to the team. This is best done on a Friday right after the sales staff meeting. In order to be considered, staff is supposed to:

1. Send JT an email describing the talk and why you think the staff would be interested
2. If approved, send an introduction email to the speaker and JT so he can coordinate timing for the next available week
3. We will hold 2 Fridays a month for internal speakers or breaks, so don’t expect to see a speaker on the calendar every week
4. Presentations will be limited to 30 minutes in almost all circumstances. Those who desire can stay afterwards for continued discussion

   1. If you think a particular speaker merits a longer timeframe, mention that in the initial email and we can consider it.

Once in contact with a speaker, you should:

1. Confirm with the presenter their availability and match to the SA calendar
2. Shorten the staff meeting to 30 minutes (9:00-9:30 Mountain)
3. Create a new event for the speaker starting at 9:30 mountain
4. Invite the staff and the speakers to the newly scheduled event
5. Record the presentation and save at [Staff Meetings](https://datacentric.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/staff/EvJVzeq_ucJAk9rhp8ANE8oBsNg9jqqDVhGJQqgdZCIjyQ?e=aQRKJA)

Sometimes, these Friday speakers will be internal. Some potential topic areas might be:

1. Administrative or HR topics
2. Presentations from developers on tools
3. Presentations from ontologists on projects or methodology

Internal Presentations should be recorded and distributed as well. Try to rotate internal and external presentations to keep things fresh. You should still also set up the 2nd meeting as you did with an external speaker. Even though you will probably not actually use it, this is a good reminder there is a presentation that day for scheduling purposes.
