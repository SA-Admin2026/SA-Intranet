Each week, I prepare a report for Dave regarding business operations. A few notes below

### Section 1: Key Dates

I always include the next payroll, then generally the next week worth of important stuff. If there are bigger events coming soon (DCA Forum or something), I start adding them maybe a month out.

### Section 2: Operations/General

Report on overall business health type items or misc. work I’m doing. This usually includes a staff utillization number as well

### Section 3: Recruiting

Current year added/lost as well as any pipeline updates or people pending onboarding.

### Section 4: Sales & Marketing

Get the metrics information from Pipedrive (Insight section). Make sure to indicate the +/- on # of deals in each stage.

### Section 5: Financials

All this is pulled from QuickBooks - generally from beginning of the year up to today. Pay attention specifically to overdue invoices. If there are some, you should also include what you are doing to action those to get them paid promptly.
