## **Credit Card**

### Recording Receipts

Dave is the only one with a company credit card, receipts will arrive via email or on the desk.

* In QB, go to the section to enter Credit Card charges
* Insert Vendor, Date, and Amount as shown on the receipt.
* Enter the correct account (typically a subaccount of 430).
* Enter a memo that is short/to the point.
* Be sure to enter the correct Class and Job.
* Enter under the appropriate card under the CC Receipts section of QB.

  + Make sure to enter under the date it was charged to make reconciliation easier.
* Print receipts and place them in the appropriate monthly file in Dave's office once entered into QB.

### Reconcile Statements

* Dave will leave a copy of the statement on JT’s desk or will receive it in the mail.
* Open the QB reconciliation option and go through all transactions. Place a check next to the ones accurately recorded in QB and circle those that are not reflected in QB.
* If possible, reconcile the ones you know about or have records for.
* Place the marked-up copy of the bill in Dave's office, in the "To Be Reconciled" folder.

## **Expensify Reports**

* Typically received via Expensify or email.
* Validate the expense, checking with Dave if necessary.
* Download the Expensify reports with all attached receipts.
* Print a copy and leave for Dave in the AP/AR folder for final review
* Add expense amount to Benefits Spreadsheet in the Payroll folder
* At Payroll time, add all expenses as pre-tax expense reimbursement
* Approve expense on Expensify or respond to the email notifying the staff worker it's been submitted.
* If the expense relates to the Training and Learning Budget, see [Training & Learning](https://semarts.atlassian.net/wiki/x/CIBehg)
