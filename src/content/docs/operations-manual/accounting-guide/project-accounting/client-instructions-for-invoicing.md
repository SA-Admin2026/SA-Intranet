# General Information

All projects require the following information

1. Contract Setup: Does the client issue a Purchase Order, other contract info, require vendor onboarding, a Master Services Agreement, etc. If a PO is required, always include the PO # on the invoice.
2. Onboarding: What is the process for vendor onboarding and getting our consultants setup with their systems.
3. Time Entry: If applicable, how do consultants enter time in their systems?
4. Invoice Submission: How do we submit invoices? Milestone, Hours, Expense, etc.

   1. Advisory Rates, Write-offs, and fixed fees – Pay attention to which contract is milestone/fixed fee (where the invoice total is a fixed amount and based on a schedule), if it advisory (rates are ~30% higher than regular rates), and if there’s a write-off (a discount to the rates or overall project fees).
5. Payment Remittance: How is payment of invoices by our clients received? When you receive them, print them out and leave in AP/AR folder

Video Tutorial (Ariba): <https://youtu.be/6tx7b-PFZc0>
