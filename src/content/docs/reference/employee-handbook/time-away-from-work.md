## Time Away From Work

 

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

 Paid time off is not considered a form of additional wages, but part of the Company’s commitment to employee health and flexibility. Semantic Arts encourages the use of paid vacation time to recharge and avoid burnout. In order to promote this, we have a flexible paid vacation policy. Full time employees may schedule and take PTO as you like, subject to management approval. You should also be sensitive to project deadlines and meetings as it relates to your time off, along with communicating clearly with others on the team to ensure tasks and responsibilities are accounted for. This is not one of those “unlimited” vacation policies that are well intentioned and yet lead to employees actually using less vacation time out of fear of job security. We would encourage employees to aim for approximately 4 weeks of vacation per year. We will run a query on a monthly basis to review vacation time utilized and make recommendations as appropriate. This query will run over the past 18 pay periods and look for roughly 6 weeks of vacation over that time. The reason for the longer lookback is to account for spikes due to longer trips and end of the year travel. The intent of time off is to re-charge your batteries, therefore we encourage you to take it in full day increments when you are not also working. In general, you should charge a full day’s worth of hours of PTO on a scheduled day off and no hours of work. There can be exceptions when you start PTO at mid-day, but these should be the exception. Employees should not work during their PTO. When you schedule PTO, we ask that you commit to returning for at least as long as your vacation (e.g., if you schedule a six-week vacation, you are expected to return to work for at least six weeks after the vacation). Because this is not an accrual-based policy, no PTO will be paid out upon separation from the company. In the normal course of business, we will not be approving (or not approving) requests for time off. We expect you will make sure to cover any required duties between others on your projects, clients, and any other obligations. Notification of upcoming time off is completed via the procedure found in the Employee Reference Guide. If we detect a pattern of excessive time off, you will be sent an email requesting you curtail your PTO for a season of time. 

## Sick Time

 We understand that unexpected absences happen, thus employees are granted a maximum of 6 days (48 hours) of sick time per calendar year. This time does not accrue year over year. Employees should use this time if they are ill or need to take an unexpected absence, or for planned medical appointments, etc. This time is available effective your first day of employment. Please note your sick leave can be used for any reason covered under the Healthy Families and Workplaces Act (Paid Sick Leave under the Colorado Healthy Families and Workplaces Act (HFWA) | Department of Labor & Employment), no matter what state you may reside in. If your resident state has a Paid Sick Time policy, any additional covered reasons for leave also apply. Employees should take no less than hourly increments of sick time and are expected to make the appropriate channels aware of their absence. No-call-no-shows and other abuse of this policy will be addressed on a case-to-case basis. You may be required to provide proof of your illness. 

## Holidays

 The office will be closed, and employees will be paid for the following 8 federal holidays: · New Year’s Day · Memorial Day · Independence Day · Labor Day · Thanksgiving Day & Friday after Thanksgiving · Christmas Eve Day & Christmas Day Please note, employees are free to modify these holidays to fit your circumstances, as long as no more than 8 holidays are taken paid per year. For example, those residing in Canada may choose to forego the Independence Day holiday and instead apply that day to a local holiday. Generally, a holiday that falls on a Saturday will be observed on the Friday previous, and those falling on a Sunday will be observed the following Monday. Specific notices about holidays will be emailed by the office as needed. You should utilize the appropriate holiday entry in the timecard system to document these dates. 

## Bereavement

 Full-time employees are eligible for leave due to the death of either an immediate or extended family member and related activities. You are permitted to utilize either sick time or PTO to cover your absence during these times or may choose to take leave unpaid. Be sure to communicate your absence through the usual channels and with those required to cover your duties while you are away. Should you need any additional resources, the EAP (mentioned in the benefits section) is available for counseling related to mental health, financial health, and more. Contact HR for further information if needed. 

## Jury Duty

 Semantic Arts realizes that it is the obligation of all U.S. citizens to serve on a jury when summoned to do so. All employees will be allowed time off to perform such civic service as required by law. Employees are expected, however, to provide proper notice of a request to perform jury duty and verification of their service. Employees also are expected to keep management informed of the expected length of jury duty service and to report to work for the major portion of the day if excused by the court. If the required absence presents a serious conflict for management, employees may be asked to try to postpone jury duty. Employees on jury duty leave will be paid for their jury duty service in accordance with state law; however, exempt employees will be paid their full salary for any week in which time is missed due to jury duty if work is performed for the Company during such week. 

## Witness Service In the event you receive a subpoena to testify, assist, or participate in any court proceeding or administrative proceeding (e.g., the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, Fair Employment and Housing Commission, National Labor Relations Board, etc.), the Company will approve unpaid leave for you to fulfill such civic obligations. You are required to provide a copy of the subpoena or other notice of the court or other proceeding to the Company as far in advance as possible so that appropriate scheduling adjustments may be made. Exempt employees may be required to provide documentation of any amounts paid to the employee by a court, subpoenaing party, or other source for fulfilling the duty, because amounts equal to such payments received may be deducted from the employee’s salary, consistent with applicable law. Civic Duty Leave is unpaid for non-exempt employees, and they are not required to report to the Company any payments that they may receive. 

## Voting

 Voting is an important responsibility we all assume as citizens. We encourage employees to exercise their voting rights in all municipal, state, and federal elections. Under most circumstances, it is possible for employees to vote either before or after work. If it is necessary for employees to arrive late or leave work early to vote in any election, employees should arrange with their supervisor/manager no later than the day prior to Election Day. 

## Leaves of Absence

 

## Parental Leave

 There are two types of leave available related to parental leave, paid and unpaid. Full-time company employees are eligible for two weeks of paid leave specifically related to the birth or adoption of a child (both parents) in addition to the guidelines provided for PTO. This leave does not need to be taken consecutively. For extended leave beyond these two weeks, the Company may be able to provide additional unpaid leave, as long as such leave does not create an undue burden on the Company. Such leave may be required based on the employee’s state of residence, and as such we will comply with any applicable laws. Depending on your state of residence, there may be compensation available through a state Family Leave program. Please contact HR if you have any questions about this. 

## Medical Leave An unpaid medical leave of absence of not more than 12 weeks may currently be granted to full-time employees. This unpaid leave is intended to cover any absences arising from illness, injury, or caregiving. Medical Leave may be granted if each of the following conditions is met:
- The employee has completed at least ninety (90) days of employment with our Company.
- Human Resources is notified as soon as possible by the employee of the need for medical leave.
- The employee submits to Human Resources a completed leave of absence form and/or a written statement from the healthcare provider outlining the reason for leave and the estimated time needed. (The Company reserves the right to obtain a Company healthcare provider’s opinion.)
- Approvals are obtained from the President, prior to the leave. When the estimated period of leave is less than 12 weeks, and an employee needs to extend the leave, another healthcare provider’s statement is required indicating the new estimated length of leave. An employee ready to return to work from leave must present a healthcare provider’s statement indicating ability to return to work, and if any restrictions may apply. The Company may reinstate an employee ready to return from a medical leave of absence when, in the opinion of the Company, it is practical to do so. The Company does not guarantee reinstatement of an employee to the former job. When the employee is available to return to work, the employee is free to apply for any vacancy available and may be considered along with other applicants. The Company currently continues medical insurance benefits for an employee on leave for a maximum of 12 weeks if the employee continues to pay the employee’s portion of the premium. Holiday pay and jury duty pay will not be granted during the leave. Employees who fail to return at the expiration of their authorized leave may be terminated. If the employee’s failure to return is due to a disability under the Americans with Disabilities Act or other law, additional accommodations may be provided. Employees must supply enough information from their healthcare provider indicating that they have a covered disability and indicate when they can return to work with or without reasonable accommodation. Accommodations must not cause undue hardship to the employer. Potential accommodations will be determined in an interactive process between the employee and the Company. Part-time employees are not eligible for medical leave except as required by law. 

## Military Leave

 Employees granted a military leave of absence are reinstated and paid in accordance with the laws governing veterans’ re-employment rights. 

## Other Leave

 There are various other reasons an employee may desire a leave of absence. In some cases these are governed by state or federal law, and the Company is committed to compliance with these requirements. In other cases, there may be a need or desire by the employee to take an extended leave. This should be discussed with HR or the President and are approved on a case-by-case basis.
