## Benefits Overview

 Details around Semantic Arts benefit offerings are available on SharePoint, in the public HR folder. ## Part-Time Employee Benefits

 For employees that are not scheduled to work a full-time schedule, benefits are different than those full-time. See below for a breakdown
- 401(k) match up to 4% still available
- Employees scheduled for at least 30 hours per week remain eligible for health insurance coverage
- Sick time is reduced to 3 days per year, can be taken in small increments or not charged if outside of regular working hours
- Vacation is roughly 2 weeks per year in place of 4, but can be adjusted based on supervisor feedback - Training and learning budget is available, but all expenses must be pre-approved or discussed ahead of time 

## Health Insurance

 Semantic Arts offers a group plan with options from a national provider. We subsidize a large portion of the cost for an employee, as well as some cost for dependents. These plans are shopped and compared annually to available offerings to attempt to offer the best value to our employees. Semantic Arts works hard to stay aware of the current challenges and scams that exist within the health insurance and prescription drug industries while also working with a licensed insurance advisor to ensure high quality, fair plans for employees. Overall, there are a number of different options that exist for procuring insurance. Given the overall high price of health insurance, we recommend you take the time to look at the various options that exist and make the right decision for you. We are not insurance advisors; all decisions should be made with the support of insurance professionals. If you need to make any changes to your plan during the plan year due to qualified life events (change in spousal coverage, etc.), then please start by contacting HR. We will open an enrollment option for you to make changes to your plans for a specific window. Please keep in mind many qualified life events have a set amount of time in which to make changes. Failure to notify HR in a timely manner may result in missed opportunities. Deductions from payroll for insurance contributions will be done over 12 pay periods from January, withholding the estimated yearly cost evenly divided. The last payroll before the new plan period will be used to reconcile the year’s payments to match what was actually due to the insurance provider. This means generally there will be no deduction on that 13th payroll. If reconciliation requires additional funds, you will be notified before the payroll is run. 

## Prescription Drug Coverage

 Semantic Arts plans include some coverage for prescription drugs. Many companies utilize what is called a "PBM" to help manage drug coverage with a claim of "lower costs", but due to highly unethical practices this is landing more and more organizations in court and leaving employees with medications sometimes at a 2400% cost increase! We do not use a PBD, and therefore avoid any of these unethical practices. As an alternative, we recommend whenever purchasing a new medication, you consider the use of a discount card such as GoodRx (https://www.goodrx.com/) or even asking about savings for cash pay. By comparing these costs you can often reduce the overall cost of prescription medications. ## Time Off Policies

## Workers’ Compensation

 On-the-job injuries are covered by Semantic Arts’ Workers' Compensation Insurance Policy, which is provided at no cost. If employees are injured on the job, no matter how slightly, they should report the incident immediately to their supervisor. Failure to follow Company procedures may affect the ability of employees to receive Workers Compensation benefits. This is solely a monetary benefit and not a leave of absence entitlement. Employees who need to miss work due to a workplace injury must also request a formal leave of absence. See the Leave of Absence sections of this handbook for more information. 

## Employee Assistance Program

 The EAP is available to all employees enrolled in our health plan. This provides employees and their families with information and assistance on a wide range of topics and issues including work stress, debt problems, family issues, relationship worries, parenting challenges, anxiety, grief, and much more. The program includes up to six in-person or online counseling sessions at no cost, and help to navigate any longer-tern needs. This is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. More information can be found in the Benefits Book on SharePoint. 

## Health Savings Account

 For employees with H.S.A. approved plans (either obtained through the company or individually), we can withhold H.S.A. contributions from payroll pre-tax and deposit into an individual H.S.A. account. This account must be opened with our approved bank.

## k) Retirement Program

 Offered through Human Interest, Semantic Arts has a group 401(k) plan and provides up to a 4% match on employee contributions. Employees pick the fund allocations and control their account and maintain control even after separation from the company. There is no vesting or contribution limits outside of required IRS regulations. Employees will be automatically enrolled upon hire unless indicating otherwise. Upon separation from the company, you retain control of this account to either continue to contribute or rollover. 

## Student Loan Reimbursement

 The company will provide matching funds for the repayment of student loans up to $100 per month. To qualify, the employee should provide proof of payment (receipts, canceled checks, etc.) of a maximum of $100/month up to the number of months employed that year, by December 1st. The Company will compensate the employee a matching amount in the last pay period of the year. If you wish to participate in this program, please fill out the form available from HR and submit the form and proof of payment. Note: If the employee’s start date falls before the 15th of the month they start, it counts as a full month. If they start after the 15th, the count does not begin until the following month.

## Employee Development Benefits

 Semantic Arts believes strongly in investing time and resources into our employees. This includes $3,000 set aside annually for an employee’s self-directed skill development. Expenses must be pre-approved, but can often be used for classes, conferences, books, and more. In addition, those who choose to present either virtually or in-person on topics related to semantic technology are given additional budget into their fund, for use in further education or paid out as a bonus at the end of the year (subject to policy constraints). Further details can be found on Confluence in the Company Reference Guide. ## Semantic Arts Summits
