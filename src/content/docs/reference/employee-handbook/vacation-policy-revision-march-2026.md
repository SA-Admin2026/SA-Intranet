Paid Time Off Policy Philosophy & Purpose Paid time off is not considered a form of additional wages, but rather part of the Company’s commitment to employee health, sustainability, and long-term performance. We believe that employees who rest and recharge are more creative, more engaged, and more effective in their work. This is a true unlimited paid time off policy. There is no accrual, no cap, no carryover balance, and no target number of days. We trust you to take the time you need. We mean it. Because this policy does not create an accrual or determinable balance of PTO, no vacation pay will be owed or paid out upon separation from the Company, consistent with Colorado law governing unlimited PTO arrangements. Eligibility This policy applies to all full-time, exempt employees. Part-time and non-exempt employees should refer to their individual employment agreements for applicable leave terms. This policy supersedes and replaces any prior vacation or PTO policy previously in effect. Taking Time Off We encourage you to take time off. Here is how to do it well:
- Plan ahead when possible. Give your manager and teammates reasonable advance notice, particularly for extended absences. This is not a formal approval gate, it is good professional practice.
- Ensure coverage. Before you leave, coordinate with your team to ensure that client and internal commitments, deadlines, and responsibilities are covered and document s handed off where appropriate. Employees should not work while on PTO. How to Request Time Off Approval is based on business needs and reasonable scheduling—not on how much time you have taken or plan to take. The Company will not deny requests on the basis of PTO usage alone. Denials will be grounded in specific, documented business needs, and the Company will work collaboratively with employees to find alternate dates whenever possible. Our Encouragement to You We want you to take vacation, and to take enough of it. Research consistently shows that employees who take regular time away are more productive, more creative, and less prone to burnout. This is good for you, and it is good for the Company. Your manager may periodically check in with you if it appears you have not taken time off in an extended period, not to impose a limit or evaluate your performance, but to genuinely encourage you to use this benefit as intended. We also encourage managers to model healthy time-off behavior. Leaders who visibly take vacation create the cultural permission for their teams to do the same. Policy Integrity This policy is genuinely unlimited. There is no formal ceiling on the number of days an employee may take, and the Company will not track, benchmark, or publish usage metrics in a manner that implies a limit. However, the unlimited nature of this policy comes with shared responsibility. Employees are trusted to exercise good judgment. Patterns of absence that interfere with job performance, client obligations, or team operations may be addressed through the Company’s standard performance management process. Abuse of this policy, such as frequent unscheduled absences or absences that cause material disruption without adequate notice or handoff, may result in a performance conversation or corrective action under standard HR procedures.
