:::note[Handbook vs. Reference Guide]
The **Employee Handbook** sets out the essential *rules and requirements*. The **Company Reference Guide** is the companion: less about rules, more about *how to navigate* the day-to-day. When a policy in the Handbook applies to your state, the **state addendum** governs.
:::
