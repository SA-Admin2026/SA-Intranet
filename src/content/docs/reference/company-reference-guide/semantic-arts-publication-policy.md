# Semantic Arts Publication Policy

The purpose of this policy is to ensure the protection of Semantic Arts intellectual property, the maintenance of our standards of quality, and uniformity of messaging.

You must obtain approval from the President or CEO before making any Semantic Arts work products public. The President/CEO will enlist others in the review as appropriate.

This policy encompasses:

- Public GitHub repositories
- Publications
- Conference presentations
- Code, ontologies, or data models that are not already public
- Slide decks or materials posted on public websites
- Blog posts, articles, or white papers
- Contributions to external publications or newsletters
- Recorded talks, webinars, or training sessions
- Discussion of company work in podcasts or interviews
- Any other public distribution of Semantic Arts work products or derivative materials

Following approval, please inform [office@semanticarts.com](mailto:office@semanticarts.com) of the work product so that it can be appropriately promoted or referenced in marketing materials.
