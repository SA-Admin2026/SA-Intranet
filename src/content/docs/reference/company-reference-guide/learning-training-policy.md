## Semantic Arts Learning/Training Policy

We are committed to the idea of bringing lifelong learning to bear on our engagements.  Also, we recognize speaking and presenting at conferences and webinars is a key part of our getting our thought leadership out there.

There are three main parts to the policy:

- Personal training budget
- Presentation incentives
- Bonus

### Personal Training Budget

Each employee has an annual training budget of $3,000.  It can be used to cover conference attendance, online training, or college tuition along with any associated travel expenses. It can also be used to purchase items like books and software. It does not roll over from one year to another.

All requests for use of your training budget must be pre-approved by your direct supervisor:

- Technical staff (consultants and developers): President
- Non-technical staff (generally): COO

Requests should be submitted through the [SharePoint training budget request form](https://datacentric.sharepoint.com/:u:/s/staff/IQAXdjTLIq_5R7D7GvLnAA6FARn6t8TU2sb-I2i-2yPYf4M?e=FGaCRt). Please submit your request at least one week before you need to commit the funds.

For technical staff, we will generally approve anything that has direct bearing on our practice, including both semantics and software development. We also support branching out to adjacent disciplines like artificial intelligence or to specialized domains that intersect with our practice, such as pharma and cybersecurity.

Requests by non-technical staff will be considered for relevance to their work, but we also encourage training in semantics and knowledge graph fundamentals to gain a better understanding of the practice your work supports.

Failure to obtain express consent before an expenditure could result in the firm declining to reimburse those expenses.

Please also note that materials purchased with this budget are the property of Semantic Arts, and the company retains the right to require return at any time.

Reimbursement requests are subject to the [expense reimbursement policy](travel-expense-policy.md#Expenses), including the requirement that expenses must be submitted within 45 days of occurrence in order to be reimbursed. Employees may also be asked to provide a debrief of the event/training and lessons learned so that the entire company can benefit.

### Presentation Incentives

In many cases, we have the opportunity to speak at conferences.  Speaking will increase the annual value of your training budget as follows:

- $25 for each live attendee at your talk, $10 for each virtual attendee
- $100 for each contact acquired from a live presentation (if we don’t already have that lead captured)
- $10 for each attendee at an online function (such as gistCouncil or EPG)

So, if 100 people were in the audience and you got 10 leads, your training budget would increase by $ 3,500 ($25\*100 + $100 \*10).  Your new training budget would be $6,500.

Often speaking comes with a waiver of the conference fee, so you won’t have to deduct that from your budget.  Occasionally speaking has a stipend or honorarium associated with it.  Have this made out to Semantic Arts and add this to your budget.  If the above scenario had a $500 stipend your budget would be up to $7,000.

You may continue to take training until you exhaust your budget (subject to approvals in the procedure above)

### Bonus

At the end of the year, we review your budget, revised upward based on your incentives, and subtract all training related expenses. From this figure we subtract the original budget ($3,000) and pay you half the difference.

## Procedures

### Accounting

Make sure all expenses related to training or learning are so marked when you submit them to the Office Manager utilizing Expensify where possible. It is also your responsibility to notify the office manager if you speak at a conference, including how many attendees/leads there were.

### DCAF and Staff Summits

The summer Data-Centric Architecture Forum is considered training and will be charged against your training budget. The same presentation incentives apply, however there are no credits for “leads” as we already have counted them by nature of their attendance.

The staff internal summit is not charged against individual training budgets.
