Semantic Arts, Inc. wants to make it easier for our employees to have the necessary devices to excel in their positions.  As such, the company will purchase a device on the employee’s behalf with the following provisions:

**Policy Provisions**

1. Semantic Arts will purchase a computer, as specified:

   - Employee will pay half the amount of the device through a payroll deduction
   - Employee may select the computer to meet their work needs
   - Technical staff should ensure to select a device with enough RAM to support heavier computing
   - Only one device may be purchased at a time
2. Semantic Arts owns the device and will claim its depreciation.
3. Semantic Arts reserves the right to perform a security audit of the employee’s device at any time.
4. After a period of 12 months of continuous employment, the asset then transfers to the employee.
5. New devices may be purchased every three years, or with a clear business need if before then.  Please contact [office@semanticarts.com](mailto:office@semanticarts.com) for review and approval.
6. Employee authorization is required to participate.

In order to participate:

1. Email [office@semanticarts.com](mailto:office@semanticarts.com) to confirm eligibility. Include a link to the exact device you wish to purchase. The Operations Team will purchase the device using a company card and have it shipped directly to your home
2. After purchase, the Ops Team will notify you of the exact price after tax and shipping. You should then complete the BYOD form (found on SharePoint within the Public HR folder) and return to office@semanticarts.com
3. The appropriate amount will be deducted from your next payroll
