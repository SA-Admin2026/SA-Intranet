Given the company’s commitment to remote-first employment, we want to support employees in maintaining a productive work environment and to effectively perform job duties outside of a corporate office.

To that end, we are going to pilot the idea of providing funds towards some home office expenses going forward, with some specific guidelines outlined below:

1. Requested expenses will be approved on a case-by-case basis. The company reserves the right to decline funding for any reason, including to protect the health of company financials.

2. Expenses must be reasonable – don’t spend more money on something just because it’s coming from our pocket and not yours. Similar to our travel guidelines, spend company money as if it were your own.

3. Types of expenses we will consider for reimbursement include less expensive monitors, keyboard/mouse, webcams, headsets, inexpensive furnishings, etc.

We will not be reimbursing for home internet costs or other utilities, except where required by law.

4. More expensive items such as computers will still be handled via the BYOD program, where we split the cost of the item and there are limitations regarding the frequency of new purchases.

For approved expenses, the COO will purchase the item with a company credit card and have it shipped directly to your house.
