If you're contemplating using AirBnB for a business trip, here are a couple of pointers.

First do a search on a normal web site like hotels.com and get an idea of what is available in the traditional market.  I don't want to have to explain why we paid more than the going rate for an airbnb.  If there is any question, save a screen shot of the most reasonable selection of hotels you could have gotten for that price.  Note that some sites are quoting per night costs and some for the whole stay.  Some (hotwire) don't show taxes and fees until you're ready to pay (adding about 20%).  Airbnb is also not showing cleaning and other fees until you get to checkout, often 15-20%

Type in either the city or the exact address, either way you're going to be doing a map based search. You want to check some check boxes such as "entire home" (which means entire appt in the case that its an appt.)  You might occassional want a "private room" but keep in mind this is a room in someone's home, you don't have your own entry.  I can't imagine anyone taking the "shared room" option.

Also you will want to fiddle with the filters (price, number of bedrooms, wifi, there isn't a check box for airconditioning, so might want to check the write up.

There are two kinds of offerings: instant book and regular.  Instant book works pretty much like a hotel.  You click and they accept (although occasionally they don't, weirdly).

Instant booking does not work on same day.

Check the reviews.  I'm very skeptical about any place without reviews.  I'm seeing a new trend which is "contact me directly" I suspect this is a scam, and if something goes wrong I doubt that airbnb will help you.

If you don't find an "instant book"  don't "request to book" the person has 24 hours to say yes or no, and that is time you can't place another booking (cuz they might say yes and then you'll have two).  Instead, click the "contact host" toward the bottom of the page and say something like "planning on coming xx/xx/xx to xx/xx/xx, wanted to double check that the place is available"  that way you can put several of those out there.

Also have a look at the "cancellation policy" they range from liberal to strict, which might be important, depending on how certain you are.
